Bando

An FPV drone simulator built for freestyle and racing, set in abandoned factories, a derelict city, a sports centre and an empty flat.

  • Acro and angle modes with adjustable rates, FOV and camera angle
  • Gamepad, radio transmitter and keyboard support
  • Six locations with gated racing tracks and saved best times
  • Freestyle lines through hoops, pipes, frames and broken windows
  • In-game map builder with hundreds of props, and map sharing
  • Drone builder: frames, motors, props, stacks, cameras, batteries

pitchRC

A physics-focused RC flight simulator with fixed-wing aircraft, helicopters, guided lessons, landing challenges and three flying sites.

  • 15 aircraft: trainers, aerobatic planes, bush planes, racers, a biplane, an airliner, fighters and helicopters
  • Start with the Trainer TR-24, then unlock more by earning coins
  • Transmitter setup: stick mode, rates, expo, trims, reversing and mixes
  • Choose your map, spawn point and weather
  • Keyboard or gamepad controls
