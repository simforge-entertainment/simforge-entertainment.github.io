Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

This policy applies to all apps published by Simforge Entertainment ("Simforge", "we"), a sole proprietorship based in Finland.

Information we collect

Our games do not require an account, and we do not ask for or collect your name, email address, precise location, contacts or similar personal information. Game progress and settings are stored on your device. We do not run our own servers that receive your data.

Advertising and purchases (pitchRC on Android)

The Android version of pitchRC may show ads through Google AdMob and may offer in-app purchases through Google Play. AdMob may collect and use device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), IP address and general device and usage information to serve and measure ads, and to show personalised ads where permitted. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation in your Android settings (Settings > Google > Ads). See Google's Privacy Policy and how AdMob uses data. Purchases are processed entirely by Google Play; we never receive your payment details.

This website

This website does not use cookies or analytics. The pitchRC section can show a YouTube video. Nothing is loaded from YouTube until you press play, at which point the video is served by YouTube in its privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com) and Google's Privacy Policy applies to it. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log visitors' IP addresses as described in GitHub's privacy statement.

Other games

Bando and Fighter Combat do not include advertising. If this changes, this policy will be updated before the change is released.

Children

Our games are not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

Data deletion

Because data stays on your device, uninstalling the app removes it. If you have questions about any data we may hold, contact us and we will respond promptly.

Changes

We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest revision.
